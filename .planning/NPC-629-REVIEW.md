## Why preceding proofs missed these failures

- The small queued publication fixture used a few native rows and delivered
  complete buffers directly to the receiver. It did not establish latency through
  the actual mixed publisher, fragmentation, complete-picture admission and
  rendering under the heterogeneous prepared-module load.
- The existing printed-area proof compared a native enhancement holder without
  its independently published physical-face module. It could not detect the
  hover-only update restarting one root's interpolation halfway through a turn.
- The preceding flight audit correctly traced shared easing and endpoints, but
  treated purchase, sale and cancellation as one release path. That source path
  itself returned a sold item to its former fan. Transport parity cannot correct
  an inappropriate local destination.

## Standing presentation contracts

Original native widgets, texts, option rows, card art, materials, selection and
hover state remain the owner's presentation. Images are not sent over the wire;
peers resolve original asset identities and apply owner-authored property data.
Visibility is not an excuse to omit a currently visible row or use a replacement
widget. An originally invisible branch need not block a visible picture, but
must become a complete exact dependency when the owner actually shows it.

The explicit visitor-local pre-drop card and purse guides remain the sole town
guide exceptions. Actual offered/held/returning objects, options, confirmations,
resident poses and audio remain shared. Map cards stay public. Scenario card
privacy, the accepted temple return and blessing path, and Frame628 rendering
controls are outside the changes in this round.
