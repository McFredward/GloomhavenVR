## Hardware evidence

The owner's `Player.log` records a remote enhancement picture requiring 44 of
66 prepared modules. It received 20 after 9.017 seconds and admitted the picture
after 28.557 seconds. The visitor's publication trace accounts for those 44 cold
modules and 248,474 serialized bytes. Those are original-property metadata, not
image pixels. `LogOutput.log` omits the Debug-severity traces that `Player.log`
retains; an absent trace in the former is not evidence of an unexecuted probe.

The source still disables sparse native-template encoding for active enhancement
and merchant confirmation originals. The previous visible census also treats
active but non-rendering native card partitions as visible dependencies. Thus
Build629's smaller census does not eliminate the cold transfer bottleneck.

The original orientation screenshot shows a card nearly edge-on while the aura
and selected regions face forward. The old test represents the holder as one
small module. Real publication separately partitions the holder, native ring and
enhancement regions, so correcting the holder alone is insufficient evidence.

The visitor's native Temple opens and closes five frames later. The owner also
switches immediately from Temple to Enchantress. The purse currently depends on
that native window's lifetime, while the resident's attention range exceeds the
approach and departure gates. A priestess looking at a visitor therefore does
not prove that this visitor's purse producer still exists.

## Review boundaries

Every owner-visible original, property, placement, hover, scroll, clock, card
flight and sound remains shared. Only the previously approved town pre-drop
guides remain visitor-local. The 3D map remains public. Native observer clones
must be inert, and no presentation change may manufacture game state.

Delivery must retain exact owner values without assuming matching localized
observer defaults. The original network bandwidth and continuous avatar lane
remain bounded. Geometry proof must include independent descendant modules,
native canvas frames and reordered arrival, not just a root quaternion. Purse
proof must cover approach, departure, another resident, character ownership and
the actual wrist producer; a changed focus boolean is insufficient.

The maintainer additionally questions unnecessary hidden flat-window work.
Original service callbacks are required for the native transaction rules; that
does not authorize rendering, repeatedly rebuilding or polling unused window UI.
Review this separately from the purse visibility repair and distinguish retained
logical controllers from owner-visible original inscriptions and controls.

## Integrated implementation

Build632 starts from the current combined dev tree, including Frame630 and
card-filter631. It adds no asset bank, graphics default or NPC animation redesign.

- **First picture:** record110 pools exact original scalar/string/property/node
  values across a bounded coherent transaction before envelope compression. Its
  current visible census and originals travel atomically. It needs no preceding
  dictionary and does not depend on observer-localized defaults. Image pixels do
  not cross the wire. The existing global event budget, fair lanes and continuous
  avatar stream remain unchanged.
- **Visible dependencies:** actual native ink and its ancestor mounts determine
  admission. Empty holder/tooltip transforms, culled graphics, inactive canvases
  and blank native labels cannot delay a visible transaction. Mesh visibility is
  independent of CanvasGroup alpha. Cancellation also updates the census when
  module membership remains unchanged.
- **Native geometry:** every independently published offered-card descendant
  receives the owner's print-relative frame, in parent-before-child order.
  Remote ring/selection regions use that same original frame, without a second
  observer-facing transform. Individual native node clocks prevent a repeated
  print/header update from restarting an unrelated ring tween.
- **Quiet services:** ordinary immersive visits at all three residents no longer
  dispatch native map/window entry. Merchant/mage prepare their exact original
  transaction source; temple prepares its original model, slots and book
  counters. The selected owned character remains selected. Original callbacks,
  stock, ownership, gold, enhancement and donation validation remain authoritative.
  Unchanged prepared contexts reuse their widgets; counters refresh only when
  their model changes. This removes flat window rendering and entry/list/portrait
  animation work; it does not remove the original widgets the immersive player
  actually needs to see or interact with.
- **Purse:** actual resident proximity, current owner and wrist/held/parked purse
  intent determine its producer lifetime. Closing an unrelated native service
  window cannot erase it. The actual wrist reveal still controls when it is drawn;
  affordability affects the drop permission, not its ability to be held.
- **Exit and failures:** explicit immersive-mode disablement restores the exact
  current original window once. Preparation failures have a bounded original
  window escape path instead of swallowing continuation. Early Reset releases
  source leases, even before a converted surface has been adopted. Multiplayer
  proxy refreshes are exact-owner and native-action scoped; presentation failures
  cannot replay payments or escape into native desynchronization handling.

## Validation

The complete integrated local gate runs once on final production source. Older
fixtures that relied on removed shared clocks, empty invisible Buttons or the
first of multiple lane manifests are repaired without removing their assertions.
The original failures remain in the validation ledger; only affected scopes are
continued. Source gates, strict Release and compiled631-to632 review are separate
evidence.

Final coverage is recorded in `.planning/debug/npc632/validation-ledger.json`:
146 local scopes, 136 direct passes and ten affected continuations, with their
original failures retained. The complete attempt took 1162.9s at eight jobs.
Already-green unrelated scopes and causal controls were not repeated. All 14
source gates, strict Release with zero warnings/errors, committed bundle/66-part
mesh-bank validation and five English/German documentation pairs pass.

The original wire entry initially stopped at its frozen test-runner inventory
before scheduling a second local run. Its inventory now includes both new local
scopes; 18 runner tests pass with two platform skips. The remaining original
golden executable passes 296,631 assertions. This is exactly 13,823 fewer than631
because the improved transport executes fewer repeated delivery iterations;
281,276 non-transport assertions are unchanged, no case/assertion was removed,
and 288 independent110 vector assertions were added. The separate portable town
codec additionally passes 12,975 pool assertions, 50,277 existing assertions and
153 grant assertions. See the retained per-case wire count audit.

Actual compiled631-to632 review finds 30 intended type differences: seven solely
inlined build constants, NetProtocol build/110, and 22 implementation/new helper
types. All 56 old WorldUI patch registrations retain their relative order, with
five new quiet proxy hooks. There is no unexplained compiled change. No production
source changed during the test-only continuations. Automated source/Unity proof
does not establish hardware acceptance.
