## Evidence and causal boundary

The supplied November 5 hardware logs identify Build 759. Original confirmations
open at four zoom metrics (1.17, 24.88, 8.92 and 2.77); the small pixel-host
metrics fall below Unity's raw-cross normalization threshold. The previously
repaired late ownership blackout does not recur. Pinned originals and hashes
are in ignored `debug/popup760/hardware759/`.

The actual Unity engine returns a zero vector for the old small-area cross.
That reaches both eye resolves and the head carrier. For original flat UI that
does not write sampled depth, reconstructed plane depth then divides 0 by 0.
This is a source-proven invalid-depth defect matching the reported scale
boundary. A Linux OpenGL image alone cannot prove the Windows D3D11 result.

The correction normalizes finite basis directions with explicit scale-free
rescaling before taking and normalizing their cross. It preserves winding,
rotation, nonuniform scale and shear. All direct consumers share this helper.
See [the mathematical review](POPUP-SCALE-760-MATH.md) for the exact audit.

## Why earlier automated evidence missed it

The earlier Popup759 fixture did use the faithful native pixel/world metric.
Its RGB-only glyph check had no world-depth occluder and did not require a
finite unit plane normal or finite reconstructed depth. Some OpenGL output can
retain color despite that invalid depth. It also created ownership before its
first capture, instead of exercising the complete ESC-to-confirmation lifecycle.
Those are limitations of the old oracle, not proof that the reported headset
picture was correct. New consumer checks must explicitly close these gaps.
