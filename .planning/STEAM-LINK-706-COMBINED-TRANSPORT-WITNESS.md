The overlay footprint lies well inside the carrier's RGB rectangle. If the PC
flattens it there, it changes RGB contribution but cannot change the zero mask.
Opaque black still contributes zero RGB and cannot hide a bright camera. If
inner opaque black hides a moving real bright hand/object while its neighboring
inner transparent lane shows that same object, independent coverage survives
under this exact condition. Color/translucency alone is not a positive witness.

Keep the head still and the local Steam dashboard closed during that observation.
Large movement can invalidate the nominal margin through PC pre-warp/clipping;
the fixture does not establish all such bounds. Wrong eye marks, a fully opaque

## Decision after the actual witness

- A positive independent-alpha witness permits development of actual dynamic
  scene surfaces on this route. Delivered detail, per-frame eye/pose/frustum
  registration, latency, native reprojection and stereo publication still need
  proof before replacing the current renderer.
- Flattening rejects this shortcut. A separate compressed coverage channel then
  requires a verified same-video session/frame/eye token and exact PC warp
  registration, not a latest-mask or approximate-time join. That is a larger
  transport change and must retain the ordinary RGB/foveation path.
- Background/API refusal is a startup failure, not proof of flattening. It must
  leave the current game intact and retain its bounded actual failure metadata.
