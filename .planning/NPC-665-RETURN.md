## Cause and repair

The private enchantress service publishes the actual owner face and backing under
its private service/session/module identities. `TownServiceSync.TickStockCore`
publishes those same native source transforms under separately allocated Stock
identities, first as hidden prepared originals and then as a native113 return.
The existing observer independently built both lanes. A complete113 activated the
Stock copies; the old private Pending/census could continue rendering or rebuilding
the offered originals until its independent withdrawal arrived. Thus correct
individual motion clocks did not ensure one physical visible card.

The new optional22-byte record116 supplies exact immutable private provenance:
source service3, session, module, structure, public claim0 and native preparation
revision. Ownership comes from the transport peer. Capture declares this relation
only when the actual native sampler target is VRCard and the two owner bindings
are reference-identical. Addresses, card IDs and matching models never authorize
transfer. The expected flight revision is exactly the native next nonzero revision
(including uint-Max-to1); an inactive false sampler still supplies its actual
preparation revision.

Before atomic113 activation, the observer validates all destination members and
origins, detaches all source parts before any parent disposal, and rekeys the actual
source RemoteModule into Stock. Its Host and Binding are the same objects. Existing
object-keyed offered mounts/clocks are detached, and the return applies the exact
original113/115 geometry and native curve. A cold observer without a rendered
source uses the existing prepared Stock originals. If an existing source is known
but Stock116 is late, activation waits for the exact record rather than exposing a
second copy or guessing which card to move.

A private source withdrawal may precede the Stock datagram. Its preannounced actual
original is kept separately for at most the existing3-second stale period and
released immediately by Stock withdrawal or owner disconnect. This is a bounded
visual hold, not transport authority. A full, not-yet-activated terminal113 can
survive only an exactly unchanged newer native root recipe while that source is
still valid and its originals are cold. Changed hand, visibility, alpha, pose,
canvas, structure/session, revision or ordinary cancellation continues to cancel
it. The Stock origin survives native completion until Stock lifecycle retirement;
a pure original repair does not erase the source provenance.

Transferred originals suppress their old private Pending copies. Authoritative
private withdrawal frees transfer tombstones, retaining a bounded exact
source-sequence floor so a late old full baseline cannot respawn between a new
same-session reoffer census and its preparation header. A new native preparation
epoch supersedes old origin mappings without disposing the current card. Each
owner has at most128 origins and128 floors. Same-origin heartbeats preserve
`ReturnOriginChangedAt`; a changed epoch supplies the source-time floor used by the
separate offered-root yaw repair.

A newer private preparation can also arrive before its Stock header or ordinary
cancellation. If old113 and old Stock116 then arrive, the absence of the consumed
old origin is not proof of a cold observer: the observer has the exact same private
identity at a different epoch. This case rejects the superseded origin instead of
activating an old Stock copy. The next actual native flight2 can still rekey that
current preparation1 original normally.
