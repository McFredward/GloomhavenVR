## 2. Seven Errors that were a false alarm — fixed

```
[Error :GloomhavenVR] [Compat] MaterialLoaderHeal: giving up on renderer 'OcclusionVolume'
after 5 retries (state null-result) — it stays hidden.
Material asset(s): 0000000000000000f000000000000000
```

`MaterialLoaderHeal` was trying to repair a renderer that is **never drawn to the screen**.
`ObjectOcclusionVolume.OnEnable` hands its `MeshRenderer` to
`TilesOcclusionGenerator.AddObjectRenderer`, and the generator's only use of that list is

```csharp
// decompiled/GH.Runtime/TilesOcclusionGenerator.cs:179-181
foreach (MeshRenderer objectRenderer in m_ObjectRenderers)
    m_OcclusionBuffer.DrawRenderer(objectRenderer, m_OcclusionObjectMaterial);
```

— drawn into an occlusion buffer **with the generator's own material**. The volume's own
material is irrelevant, which is also why its reference is the built-in-resources GUID
(`0000…f000…`) that Addressables can never resolve. "It stays hidden" was the correct
outcome; the Error was the instrument crying wolf, five retries per volume, in a log with
room for fifteen mod lines.

ModBuild 335 excludes occlusion volumes by **component** (`ObjectOcclusionVolume` on the
renderer's own object, or a `TilesOcclusionVolume` above it — that class owns an array of
renderers beneath itself, which is the one case where the parent question is the right one).

verdicts are the readback path's own known blind spot. **Recorded, not chased.**
