## Narrow implementation

NativeRingClock.Draw reads the printed rectangle's actual transformed X/Y axes,
forms its orthonormal physical plane, and uses that same plane for the root fit,
initial phase measurement and each original Graphic's circular direction. Raw
pixel-unit cross products are normalized before crossing; Unity's small-vector
normalization otherwise collapses a legitimate millimetre-scale canvas normal.
No owner-facing filter, extrapolation, sample-time gate, wire bytes, widget state,
source mask, admission, body/print mount, return113/116 or depth order is changed.

The shipped Aura has exactly five nodes: Aura, Highlight, Types, Buy and Sell.
NativeTemplates.Partition only splits subtrees while their node count exceeds 32;
none of its shipped dynamic boundaries identifies those three sprites. A shipped
Aura therefore stays intact either within the highlighter part or its own Aura
part. The same cached original rate reaches all three Graphics. The hardware's
13/18 linked original modules are not evidence that the sprites individually split.
