A mod for **Gloomhaven (digital)** on PC. Not the flat game on a big screen — the scenario is a lit
diorama on the table in front of you, and you play it with your hands.

Same rules, same saves, same campaign.

## Play it together

**Multiplayer is the whole point.** Two, three, four of you around one table — a mask where a face
would be, hands that point and reach, your own control board at your side.

Lift a miniature and everyone sees it lift. Drag the story window and it moves for everyone,
because it hangs in the room, not in front of one person's eyes.

**Friends without a headset lose nothing.** They play the flat game they already own, in the same
session, and everything you do in VR reaches them as an ordinary move.

## Your hand of cards lives in your palm

Roll your wrist up and the fan opens. Pull a card out, hold it to your face to read it, drop it
into a slot on your board.

Squeeze one off the board, hold it up, resize it in mid-air, let go — it glides back to its hex.

## Make it yours

Three pairs of hands, three masks, three control boards — one dropdown each, and **the other
players see your choice**. Every one of them was modelled for this mod.

## Choose the room you play in

A candle-lit **cellar** or a moonlit **night forest** — both built by hand, with a real star
catalogue overhead, firelight, drips and quiet ambience. Something moves in both of them when you
are not looking.

<p align="center">
  <img src="docs/img/env-cellar.jpg" width="430" alt="The cellar">
  <img src="docs/img/env-forest.jpg" width="430" alt="The night forest">
</p>

**And the room answers the game.** Infuse Fire and the cellar warms to the ceiling. Ice grows frost
across the floor. Air shakes the leaves, Earth brings up green, Light lifts the whole wood — and
Dark takes it away and walks the moon into a blood-red eclipse.

<p align="center">
  <img src="docs/img/env-elements.jpg" width="860" alt="The same two rooms under Fire, Ice, Light and Earth">
</p>

**Or play in your own living room.** Mixed-reality passthrough floats the board, the table and your
control board in your real space. It is an either/or: with see-through on, the built rooms stand
down.

## What you need

**Gloomhaven (digital) for PC** (Steam or GOG) · a Windows PC that runs it · a **PC-VR headset with
two controllers**. Room-scale or standing — a small space is fine.

Built and played on a **Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop**. Setup is two zips into the game folder,
once, and one line in a text file turns it all off again.
