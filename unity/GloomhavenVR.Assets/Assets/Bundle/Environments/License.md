# Environments — third-party asset provenance

Both ambient environments (`Env_Cellar.prefab`, `Env_Swamp.prefab`) are assembled
from **CC0 (public domain)** low-poly model packs by **Quaternius** (quaternius.com).
CC0 1.0 Universal: no attribution required; noted here for provenance anyway.

## Models/Dungeon/ — Quaternius "Modular Dungeon Pack"
- Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/modulardungeon.html (downloaded FBX set)
- License: CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Only the FBX files actually placed in Env_Cellar are copied here.

## Models/Nature/ — Quaternius "Ultimate Nature Pack" (June 2019)
- Source: via OpenGameArt, https://opengameart.org/content/ultimate-nature-pack
  (author page: https://quaternius.com)
- License: CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Only the FBX files actually placed in Env_Swamp are copied here.

## Everything else
Shaders (`Env*.shader`), procedural textures (`Textures/`), generated meshes
(`Meshes/`), materials and the two prefabs are original work of this project,
generated deterministically by `Assets/Editor/BuildEnvironments.cs`.
