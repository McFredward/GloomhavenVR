## Story

enchantress and priestess. A living Brute stands within a tangible torchlit
sanctuary, with his original Inox face, exactly three horns, bone armour, right
sword and left shield. His upward reaction motivates the reverse angle: the
enormous original Grimhorn mask peers down like a god. The original fingerless
hand gently lifts him. A scale cut reveals the same sanctuary as a tabletop world.
The figure is restored before the action begins.

The table stands in the actual SwampNight conifer forest. This follows the
maintainer's moonlight correction: one cool source stays upper-right through
all sanctuary/table shots, with warm miniature torches. The native table uses
long parallel wood planks and a thick double-profile rim. The bundled unused
MapTable prefab and a generic tavern table are superseded references. Preserve
the sanctuary miniature itself; do not substitute the separate Black Barrow

Runeveil appears opposite Grimhorn, with the original ivory mask and arcane
hand pair. Only floating masks and hands represent players; no invented torsos
or robes. Cyan glyphs belong on Runeveil's glove backs, while the palms are plain
leather. His small original Vermling Mindthief becomes visible behind the column.
The two players share the same map and each has an original Steel controlboard.

A controlled-player macro shows two original blue cards placed into the two
actual central card wells: Overwhelming Assault61 on the left, Trample72 on the
right. The first remains seated while the second lands. The existing upper-right
