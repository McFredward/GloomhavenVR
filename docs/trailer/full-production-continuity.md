## Story and pacing

A city welcomes us. The merchant, enchantress and priestess appear briefly as
residents of a tactile world. We enter a complete dungeon at the living Brute's
height. His attention shifts upward. The enormous original Grimhorn looks
directly down at him; a gentle hand lift reveals that this world is a VR diorama.
The figure is returned to its original cell. Runeveil appears opposite: another
player, another pair of hands, the same adventure.

The outer reveal uses the game's **SwampNight forest**, sourced from
`Env_Swamp.prefab`, matching the existing upper-right moonlight. The actual
visible table is **GH_Map_TableTop_Lg**, with long parallel planks and a thick
double-profile rim; `MapTableLegs` supplies the runtime underside. The bundled
`MapTable.prefab` is not spawned and is not the table identity master. Keep the
approved sanctuary miniature through all these cuts; Black Barrow footage is
a separate native reference, not a replacement map. Forest-source Blender
lighting is diagnostic and does not establish a headset image.

The film then shows the core conversion rather than describing it. Grimhorn
studies original ability cards. His original fingerless hand places **two cards
in the controlboard's two card wells**. The chosen cards lead to legal native-style
movement and Brute melee. The hands make decisions; the game figures execute
them. A short priestess donation shows the same physical presence beyond the dungeon.
The companions share the table, and the title resolves: **GLOOMHAVEN VR — STEP
INSIDE**. The old spoken lines and old invitation title are retired.

## The controlboard sequence is the centerpiece

The source gameplay selects the **Steel controlboard**: two tall card wells in
the center, two rest symbols at left, three button seats at right. A wooden
source variant exists in the repository (`docs/img/board-artwork.png`, canonical
`PlayTray.prefab`, `PlayTray_prepped.fbx`, `PlayTray_albedo.png`). The asset lane
must verify the corresponding mesh/material before it becomes the hero board.
Use the verified actual **Steel** board on the native wooden scenario table.
The maintainer's table correction does not request a different controlboard.
Never invent a generic flat tray or equate a
wooden TABLE with a wooden CONTROLBOARD.

Source contract: `Slot1` is the left initiative well from the player's view;
`Slot2` is right. Preserve the entire recognizable structure and original tilt,
rest area and button bank. The two ability cards go into these wells, **never
onto the scenario floor**, between miniatures, or onto an unmarked table surface.
The card plane follows the controlboard. Do not hover both cards indefinitely
or lose the first card as the second arrives. Preserve the original confirmation
behavior if it is shown; no hand-driven instant spell after mere card contact.

The source hero print is **Overwhelming Assault**, from the actual Brute fan.
Bind that exact front/back image to the same physical card across shots. Before
claiming a particular movement/melee combination, bind the complementary
movement card and selected upper/lower actions to source evidence. Do not invent
its move count, range, damage, text or successful outcome. Movement and melee
remain legal native-style actions; the cinematic edit is not evidence of a live
recorded transaction unless native footage supplies it.

F09 is a mask/choice shot where observer-visible cards can show backs. F10 is the
controlled-player detail and may show that player's fronts. Runeveil's selection
fan, held and placed cards remain covered in observer views. Action-phase cards
can be open. Town/map cards are public. A third-person cinematic angle must not
misrepresent what a remote observer can see during selection.
