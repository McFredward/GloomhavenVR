GloomhavenVR — third-party assets shipped inside the asset bundles
=================================================================

This file lists third-party material inside gloomhavenvr.bundle and ghvr-town.bundle:
what it is, who made it, and under what terms it travels with the mod. Read the last
section before reusing bundle assets — one asset is NOT redistributable by you.

The full provenance record for the environment art — every source URL, the date each
licence was re-read at its source, and the modifications made — lives in the repository
at unity/GloomhavenVR.Assets/Assets/Bundle/Environments/License.md.

TOWN RESIDENT FACIAL GEOMETRY
-----------------------------
The articulated faces in ghvr-town.bundle use adapted MakeHuman Community / MPFB
asset data under CC0 1.0 Universal: anatomical head topology, facial deformation
targets, teeth, tongue and eye assets. The heads are fitted and textured for the
three residents; the facial rig and runtime integration are mod-authored. No MPFB
program code is embedded in the mod.

    https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2
    https://www.makehumancommunity.org/
    https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Pinned source revisions and asset hashes are recorded in the repository at
scripts/npc-face-template/PROVENANCE.json. The CC0 text is included beside this
notice in Licenses/MakeHuman-CC0-1.0.txt. This attribution covers those asset data;
it does not relicense the game's character designs or other bundled material.


THE ONE ASSET THAT IS NOT REDISTRIBUTABLE BY YOU
------------------------------------------------
The fire texture atlas (fire_atlas_alb.png) is derived from the greyscale flame and
turbulence masks of two free Unity Asset Store packages — "Free Fire VFX - HDRP" by
Vefects (asset id 239742) and "Fire 001" by N2Studio — used under the standard Unity
Asset Store EULA (https://unity.com/legal/as-terms).

That licence is non-transferable and may not be sublicensed. It permits GloomhavenVR to
embed these texels in the mod and to distribute the mod. It grants YOU nothing: receiving
this file does not give you a licence to those textures separately from the mod, and the
mod's own GPL-3.0 licence does not and cannot cover them. If you fork or redistribute
this project, carve this asset out or replace it — a blanket open-source statement over
it would be inaccurate, because there is no right to grant.

Every other third-party asset above is CC0 or public domain and carries no such
restriction. Read this section before adding any further Asset Store material.
