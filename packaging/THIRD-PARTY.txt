VR CONTROLLER MODELS
--------------------
The controller models shown during the tutorial's controls lesson (Meta Quest
Touch Plus, Pico 4, Valve Index, and the generic fallback controller) come from
the W3C Immersive Web Community Group's WebXR Input Profiles asset package:

    https://github.com/immersive-web/webxr-input-profiles
    packages/assets

They were converted from glTF to Unity meshes; their shape and textures are
unmodified.
