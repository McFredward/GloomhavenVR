**Better shared results, more reliable menus, and clearer feedback during play.**

- **Shared scenario results:** everyone sees the statistics and achievements, with
  the original reveal animations and sounds. Any VR player can scroll, move the
  window and use its continuation buttons; scrolling and movement respond smoothly.
- **Multiplayer presentation:** improved card setup when rejoining a scenario,
  corrected remote forced-discard and active-card flights, and synchronized video
  window movement and resizing.
- **Menus at every scale:** Main Menu and Exit Game confirmations remain visible
  and clickable when zoomed in. Desktop resolution, ultrawide displays and windowed
  mode no longer change VR window layout or disrupt laser input.
- **Character creation:** starter cards stay readable without the figure covering
  them; character and quest panels retain their full layout. Stray perk summaries
  and quest-readiness badges no longer linger over other pages.
- **Board controls:** easier initiative-portrait clicks, correctly positioned enemy
  information, a combat log that retains its chosen size, and glowing rest buttons
  when resting is the only available choice.
- **Mixed reality:** transparent cards and effects preserve the virtual scenery
  behind them; unwanted backings behind health-bar symbols are removed.
- **Positional sound:** continuous figure sounds follow their actual source instead
  of remaining fixed in one ear.
- **Optional help:** delayed action explanations and waiting-player indicators make
  pending decisions clearer. Compatible multiplayer sessions also support opt-in
  gameplay cheats.

**Known issue:** some players have reported multiplayer turns getting stuck after
**End Turn**. A fix for that issue is not included in this update.

Update using the **complete release archive**. All VR players in a multiplayer group
must update together; players without the mod can still join.
