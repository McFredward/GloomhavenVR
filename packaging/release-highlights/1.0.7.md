## What's new

- Campaign city events are available from the 3D map and use the same shared choice window for every multiplayer player.
- Character and quest windows remain available when switching between the 2D and 3D map.
- Map-button hints appear beside the physical 3D buttons and are mirrored to other players.
- Area effects rotate with short B/Y taps by default: B rotates clockwise and Y counterclockwise. Both movement sticks remain free. The legacy stick binding remains available in VR Options.
