Play Gloomhaven in VR with tracked hands, interactive cards, character boards,
and shared windows in multiplayer.

- Other players' boards show the original game widgets, card effects and decisions,
  with private cards concealed during scenario selection.
- Multiplayer updates do less repeated work, and boards in Follow mode move smoothly
  with their player's mask.
- Arrange your hand during selection, actions or on the map. Other players see the
  same order and insertion marker; taking a card preserves the remaining order.
- Choose your board from illustrated tiles, adjust your main controller, and follow
  the illustrated English or German playing guide.
- Install the full download over BepInEx, then use the in-game updater for future releases.

All VR players in a multiplayer session need the same mod build; flat players can join
without it. A four-player headset performance test remains outstanding. A brief board disappearance reported during a
long rest has not yet been reproduced or explained.
