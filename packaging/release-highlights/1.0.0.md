This is the final **ModBuild 504** refresh of the 1.0.0 download. Play Gloomhaven in VR with
tracked hands, interactive cards, character boards and shared windows in multiplayer.

- Other players' boards show the original game widgets, card effects and decisions, with private
  cards concealed during scenario selection.
- Multiplayer updates do less repeated work, and boards in Follow mode move smoothly with their
  player's mask.
- Arrange your hand during selection, actions or on the map. Other players see the same order and
  insertion marker; taking a card preserves the remaining order.
- Choose your board from illustrated tiles, adjust your main controller, and follow the illustrated
  English or German playing guide.
- Install the full download over BepInEx, then use the in-game updater for future releases.

Gold piles that grow from several enemy drops now show their full current value when picked up,
matching the laser hover card.

The in-game update prompt now reliably appears when a newer release is available.

All VR players in a multiplayer session need the same mod build; flat players can join without it.
A four-player headset performance test remains outstanding.

The original `v1.0.0` Git tag remains unchanged; this release asset is rebuilt from the final
1.0.0 commit on `main` so new installations and the updater receive the complete release package.
