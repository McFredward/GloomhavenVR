#### Native Steam Link passthrough

Play from your Windows PC on **Steam Frame over Steam Link**, with Gloomhaven's table and
floating windows in your real room. Native transparency preserves the game's colours and
partially transparent effects without colour replacement. The camera image stays on the Frame.

Install the separate [Steam Link Native Passthrough companion on your PC and Frame](https://github.com/McFredward/steam-link-native-passthrough#installation).
Then choose **Mixed Reality** under **VR Options → World → Scenery** and enable
**Steam Link native passthrough** below the environment choices.

The companion currently supports **Windows PCs with NVIDIA GPUs**. Check its page for compatible
SteamVR versions and current limitations; brief passthrough interruptions can still occur.
**Steam Frame standalone** keeps its built-in native passthrough and does not need this companion.

#### 3D videos

The **GloomhavenVR intro, character films, ambient films and studio logo** now have stereoscopic
depth inside the movable video window. Move and resize the window as usual while the film
retains its recorded camera view. Story playback and shared viewing remain part of the normal
game flow.

#### Fixes and refinements

- **Change your card selection freely before confirming.** Taking cards back into your hand and
  replacing them now keeps the hand, board slots and placement guides consistent, including
  after switching characters and on other players' boards.
- **Quieter feedback:** moving across a card or an action area gives one hover vibration;
  tooltip popups no longer add repeated vibrations. Short rests no longer repeatedly play the
  card-fan sound while a card burns.
- **More precise interaction:** obstacle and trap hints appear only when the laser points at
  their tile. A figure's pickup highlight now represents an available grab, including after
  changing the table's scale.
- **Health bars respect your hands and cards**, even with visibility through walls enabled.
  Bars are hidden individually where covered, without hiding or flickering unrelated bars.
- **Continue stays usable** on chest rewards and level-up announcements when the pause menu
  is independently open.
- **Merchant and mixed-reality fixes:** improved merchant stock recovery after extended play,
  corrected water rendering, and kept initiative symbols in front of their window background.
- **Steam Frame setup restores the required launch options automatically**, preserving your
  custom options and the separate flat and VR library entries.

Update with the **complete release archive**, including its VR assets. All VR players in a
multiplayer group must update together; flat players can continue without installing the mod.
