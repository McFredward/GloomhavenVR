- **Story illustrations and live character previews now have 3D depth.** Lean or move around
  the window to see the perspective change, including the back of a character. To turn a
  preview, point at the figure, hold the laser trigger and move sideways. Its position and
  window stay fixed. You can disable character depth under **VR Options → Panels → 2D screen
  → 3D character previews**.
- **Menu cards stand out from their windows.** Ability and item hover previews have stronger
  depth, and the laser lands on their raised surfaces. **More Cards** previews stay complete,
  leave their card names readable and provide the usual hover feedback.
- **Sharper, steadier character windows.** Text, buttons and figures remain clear while you
  move, with corrected character framing, tooltip placement and overlapping UI.
- **Level-up choices stay together.** New cards appear correctly oriented from the first
  frame, the selection list remains available, and confirmations appear on the window that
  opened them. Use **More Cards** on the character's Cards page to browse outside a level-up.
- **Leaving a page cancels its pending decision.** Switching tabs, characters or windows
  declines the old confirmation, including switching from Perks to Cards for the same
  character.
- **The main menu is a movable, resizable world window**, with laser and hand grabbing and
  free movement. Your chosen surroundings also appear in the main menu and on the map,
  starting after the intro. The Default environment remains a finite space you can leave.
- **More reliable shared windows.** Simultaneous grabs no longer pull players' copies apart;
  after a laser drag, a window turns toward the player who last let go. Scenario story windows
  remain usable with the laser, and story and encounter windows lose their oversized backing.
- **Town residents stay put when anyone changes surroundings.** Woodland ground meets their
  feet while their shared height stays fixed. Travel audio also avoids overlapping loops
  after an encounter.

**Known issue:** intermittent stalls can still occur on the multiplayer map.
