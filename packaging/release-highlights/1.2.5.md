**Steam Link mixed reality, clearer turn transitions, and more reliable initiative controls.**

- **Steam Frame over Steam Link:** Mixed Reality now uses Valve's native
  passthrough on supported SteamVR and SteamOS versions. No companion patch is
  needed. The legacy companion is available in Advanced settings.
- **Scenario flow:** the original turn and round announcements play in view.
  You can hide turn announcements under **Panels & readouts**. Enemy cards
  open clear of the control boards and finish their original reveal animation.
- **Initiative and targeting:** initiative portraits respond to laser clicks
  along their edges and stay visible over the control board. Multiplayer Undo
  works after confirming an element for the Minor Mana Potion.
- **Main menu:** a FrosthavenVR preview adds a 3D landscape while leaving the
  DLC offers accessible.

**Known issue:** some players have reported multiplayer turns getting stuck
after **End Turn**. This issue remains open.

Update using the **complete release archive**. All VR players in a multiplayer
group must update together. Players without the mod can still join.
