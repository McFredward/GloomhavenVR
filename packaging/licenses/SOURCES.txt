Runtime software notices shipped with GloomhavenVR
================================================

The package-specific LICENSE and Third Party Notices files below are copied from
the pinned source checkouts used by scripts/build-runtimedeps.sh. One redundant
trailing blank line is removed from the OpenXR LICENSE; its text is unchanged.
The packaging scripts only convert text encoding and line endings for Windows.
